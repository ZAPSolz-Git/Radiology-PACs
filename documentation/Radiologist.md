1.	Case Review & Acceptance
o	Radiologist will view only the cases assigned to them.
o	On opening a case, the radiologist will have an option to Accept or Reject the study.
o	If Rejected, an automatic notification will be sent to the QA team with the reason.
2.	Image Viewing & Reporting
o	Upon acceptance, the radiologist can view all assigned images.
o	Radiologist can upload the final report in PDF format.
3.	Communication
o	In-built chat functionality to communicate with:
	QA team
	Technician
	Both (group chat)
4.	Reporting Templates
o	Pre-defined report templates will be available.
o	Radiologist can add or upload custom templates from their device.
5.	In-built Reporting Editor
o	An in-built reporting module (Excel-like editor) will be available where:
	Radiologist can type the report
	Apply formatting
	Digitally sign the report
6.	Voice-to-Text Dictation
o	Voice dictation feature will be available.
o	Supports third-party microphones.
o	Spoken audio will be automatically converted into text within the report editor.
7.	Report Revision & Resubmission
o	If changes are required, radiologist can edit and re-send the report.
o	On re-submission, automatic notification will be sent to the concerned team.
8.	Access Control
o	Radiologist will have access only to studies assigned to them.
o	No visibility of unassigned cases.
